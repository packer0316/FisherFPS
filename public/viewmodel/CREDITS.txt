First-person weapon models

ak47.glb, p226.glb, arms_basecolor.webp and arms_normal.webp are from the first-person rigs distributed by cktang88/3d-fps, revision 130ac2a5c856401ec36e7d35afcfefe40c5801f4. The upstream project adapted, posed, rigged and optimized the assets.
https://github.com/cktang88/3d-fps/tree/130ac2a5c856401ec36e7d35afcfefe40c5801f4/public/assets/models/fp
https://github.com/cktang88/3d-fps/blob/130ac2a5c856401ec36e7d35afcfefe40c5801f4/README.md

Gloved arm mesh and textures: “Fps arms” by DJMaesen (bumstrum).
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fps-arms-9452ce4cddde4110a4fd73e555a8e412

https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fps-ak-74m-animations-94be8385c402474cacd39bc096c6ca14
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fps-pistol-animations-0d7a343dcb6f401197a73c91aee93f6d

AK rifle: Operation Steel Tide / AetherRadar, original project work under MIT. The upstream rifle sources are credited to nisu, taradavies and Quaternius (CC0). See the accompanying Operation-Steel-Tide-LICENSE.txt.
https://github.com/AetherRadar/operation-steel-tide

kabar.glb: “Kabar Combat knife” by Gamekorp, CC0 1.0. Converted from the author's FBX, oriented, triangulated and packaged with an optimized texture. FisherFPS lengthened and widened the blade, adjusted its normals and added a blade-tip socket.
https://opengameart.org/content/kabar-combat-knife
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

FisherFPS implements its own view framing, fingertip fitting, arm IK, magazine and bolt choreography, optic, melee swing and lighting. The knife uses both gloves of the credited arm rig, posed at runtime for an open guard and a forward grip.

knife-arms.glb: FisherFPS derivative of the above credited ak47.glb arm rig.
The complete right glove and sleeve were mirrored with their skin weights to
rebuild the left side without the upstream gun-contact dents. Joint lengths,
UVs and texture attribution are retained. Wrist volume corrections are baked
into this dedicated mesh; the original firearm assets are unchanged.
