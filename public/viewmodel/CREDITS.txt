First-person weapon models

kabar.glb: “Kabar Combat knife” by Gamekorp, CC0 1.0. Converted from the author's FBX, oriented, triangulated and packaged with an optimized texture. FisherFPS lengthened and widened the blade, adjusted its normals and added a blade-tip socket.
https://opengameart.org/content/kabar-combat-knife
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

FisherFPS implements its own view framing, fingertip fitting, arm IK, magazine and bolt choreography, optic, melee swing and lighting. The knife uses both gloves of the credited arm rig, posed at runtime for an open guard and a forward grip.

knife-arms.glb: FisherFPS derivative of the above credited ak47.glb arm rig.
The complete right glove and sleeve were mirrored with their skin weights to
rebuild the left side without the upstream gun-contact dents. Joint lengths,
UVs and texture attribution are retained. Wrist volume corrections are baked
into this dedicated mesh; the original firearm assets are unchanged.

G5 grenade: original fictional external prop geometry and animation by FisherFPS.
Uses the same credited knife-arms.glb mesh, skin weights, joint lengths and
forearm twist correctives. No additional third-party hand mesh is introduced.

  flexion, separate thumb opposition and the hand's flexor tendon paths.
  https://surgeryreference.aofoundation.org/orthopedic-trauma/adult-trauma/further-reading/hand-flexor-tendon-mechanism
- U.S. Army / DVIDS, Hand Grenade Basics, photograph by 2LT Ellington Ward,
  2023-08-14: shoulder-side preparation, bent throwing elbow, wrist/hand grip.
  https://www.dvidshub.net/image/7971959/hand-grenade-basics
- U.S. Army, 2-1 STB soldiers demonstrate proficiency at grenade range,
  2012-09-26: stance and upper-limb movement photo reference.
  https://www.army.mil/article/87970/2_1_STB_soldiers_demonstrate_proficiency_at_grenade_range/
- A quantitative taxonomy of human hand grasps (2019): spherical grasp and
  variation in joint flexion across fingers. Four separate knuckle planes and
  an opposing thumb are fitted to the fictional prop's surface.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6377750/
- AAOS, Kinesiology and Functional Characteristics of the Upper Limb:
  palm support, finger flexion and thumb opposition in spherical grasp.
  https://www.oandplibrary.org/alp/chap05-01.asp
- Close-up visual references: a spherical object cradled against the palm,
  four finger pads around one side, thumb opposing from the other.
  https://genk.vn/luu-dan-hoat-dong-nhu-the-nao-20131127232840085.chn
  https://shop.parkinsons.org.uk/products/therapy-gel-balls-green-medium
- Van den Tillaar & Ettema (2004), A Force-Velocity Relationship and
  Coordination Patterns in Overarm Throwing: upper-arm, elbow and wrist
  coordination. Used to author overlapping arm acceleration, delayed wrist
  flexion and hand follow-through; this is game animation, not motion capture.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3938059/
