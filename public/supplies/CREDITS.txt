Runtime uses scale60 * .01 * normalized model scale, at the crate base,
following its position and yaw. The existing shared Effekseer context manages
eight bounded handles; collecting, expiry and level cleanup stop these handles.

Additional ground halo, vertical locator and 12 rising motes per crate are
procedural project shaders. No extra point lights, bloom pass, or external
assets are required. Mesh/buffer data and the Effekseer effect are loaded once.
