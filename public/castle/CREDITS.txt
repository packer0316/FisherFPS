Character and combat effects supplied by the project owner:
- 鬼新娘/Eternal_Bride.c3b, original body and moon base color, normal,
  metallic, roughness and emission textures.
- Animation intervals: TMDC_Zombie/ClientCocos/ZombieLua/Define/FishDefine.lua,
  FishType.EternalBride (27), 30 FPS.
- Effect anchors, scales and rotations:
  Define/EfkSettingDefine/FishEternalBrideEfkSetting.lua.
- Soul flight: View/GameView/Fish/FishEternalBride/FishEternalBrideSoulCollect.lua.
- Purple dissolve: FishEternalBrideSprite.lua, eternalBrideDissolve.material
  and shader_3d_zombie_dissolve.frag. Same noise4.png, 3x/2x UV noise,
  0.1 edge width, RGB (0.6627, 0, 0.9216), 2.333s hold + 4s dissolve.
- fx/ contains the original Z_037 effects, Z_018_Ghost, Z_18_Sphere_Hit,
  and their texture, model and material dependencies.

1K glTF geometry and textures bundled locally. Runtime material converts
the scans to aged white plaster with dark tear staining. No network API
is needed when playing.

Architecture, vault, mouldings, chandeliers, carved scrolls, drapes,
candles, floral decay, growths, checkerboard marble shader, stained
glass, dust, mist, combat floor markers and encounter logic:
created for FisherFPS. Existing plaster/stone/wood maps retain the
Poly Haven credits in the project README.

- mourner-glass-v2.png: aged Gothic leaded glass, veiled mourner and rose tracery.
