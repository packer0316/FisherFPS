FisherFPS adaptations, also licensed CC BY-SA 4.0:
- Normalize the source mesh to a one-metre height.
- Reduce the 613,490 valid source triangles to 45,000 desktop / 22,000 mobile.
- Bake original sculpted details into a 2048px tangent-space normal map.
- Export local GLB assets with ivory stone material and embedded normal map.
- Runtime rotates and compresses depth for wall-mounted high reliefs.
